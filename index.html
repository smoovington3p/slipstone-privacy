Privacy Policy — Slipstone

Last updated: June 19, 2026.

Overview

Slipstone ("the app", "we") is a single-player puzzle game. Your game progress stays on your device. The app is fully playable offline. We collect personal data only through two third-party services, and only as described below.

What we collect and why

Game progress (on your device). Levels, stars, hearts, hints, streak and settings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob). We show ads to keep the app free. AdMob may collect a device/advertising identifier, ad-interaction and usage data, and diagnostic data, used for advertising and analytics. On iOS we ask for your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before any tracking; if you decline, you still see ads, but non-personalized ones, and your advertising identifier is not used for tracking. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases (RevenueCat). If you buy something, RevenueCat processes your purchase history and an anonymous app-user identifier to deliver and restore your purchases. We do not receive your name, card number, or Apple ID. See: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

What we do NOT do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and the app is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking to allow/deny ad tracking; Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising to reset/limit your advertising identifier.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in the App Store account settings.
  • Reinstalling the app clears on-device progress.

Data retention & security

On-device data persists until you delete the app. Third-party data is retained per the linked Google and RevenueCat policies.

Children

Not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child provided data, contact us.
